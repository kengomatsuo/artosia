# The operator window (2026-11-10)

- **MAIN-WINDOW BUTTONS NEVER TAKE KEYBOARD FOCUS (`crates/artosia/src/ui/widgets.rs`, `Btn`, 2026-11-10).** Rune presses the focused control on Space, and Space must always mean "next slide"; dialogs and editors ask for focus with `.focusable()`. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **EVERYTHING A POINTER CAN DO, KEYS CAN DO: A FOCUSED BUTTON TAKES ENTER AS WELL AS SPACE, EVERY MENU ACTION IS ALSO A COMMAND IN SEARCH, AND A LIST IS ONE TAB STOP (`ui/widgets.rs` `enter_key`, `palette.rs`, 2026-11-11, keys-only blind test).** Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **BACKSPACE BRINGS WHAT WAS SHOWN BEFORE INTO PREVIEW, AND SHIFT ENTER ADDS PREVIEW'S ITEM TO THE PLAN AND NEVER SHOWS IT (`state.rs` `open_earlier`, `main.rs` `on_key`, 2026-11-11, third keys-only blind test).** Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **A KEY IS DRAWN WHERE IT ACTS: TAB ON THE PLAN ROW IT OPENS AND BESIDE THE SEARCH'S SWITCH, THE SPACE BAR ON PREVIEW'S RINGED ROW AT A SONG'S LAST SLIDE (`ui/plan.rs` `tab_opens`, `ui/shell.rs` `Hint::ShowNext`, 2026-11-11).** Full account: [docs/ux-principles.md](../../docs/ux-principles.md)
- **A SECTION IS NAMED IN FULL ON EVERY CHIP AND WRITTEN INTO LYRICS IN THE WINDOW'S LANGUAGE; ITS LETTER IS ONLY A KEY, DRAWN AS A KEY CAP (`ui/widgets.rs` `section_chip`, `song::set_marker_words`, 2026-11-11, two native readers).** Full account: [docs/ux-principles.md](../../docs/ux-principles.md)
- **NO DIALOG ENDS IN HALF A ROW: PARTS BEFORE SCROLLING, AND A FADE WHERE SOMETHING MUST SCROLL (`ui/sheets.rs`, `theme::size::FADE`, 2026-11-11).** Full account: [docs/ux-principles.md](../../docs/ux-principles.md)
- **A REASON SHOWN TO A VOLUNTEER IS A STRING, NEVER A LIBRARY'S OWN ERROR TEXT (`crates/artosia/src/why.rs`, 2026-11-11, found by the Indonesian reader).** Full account: [docs/i18n/README.md](../../docs/i18n/README.md)
- **A STRING A VOLUNTEER READS HAS ITS ENGLISH IN `strings.rs` AND A BRIEF IN `docs/i18n/brief.json`; INDONESIAN, LIKE EVERY LATER LANGUAGE, IS A TABLE WRITTEN FROM THE BRIEFS BY A WRITER WHO NEVER SEES THE ENGLISH (`docs/i18n/id.json`, `languages.rs`, 2026-11-11, owner-directed: "STOP TRANSLITERATING").** A new text gets its brief first (`tools/check-brief.py`), key names are drawn and never translated, and a test holds the table to its briefs. Full account: [docs/i18n/README.md](../../docs/i18n/README.md)
- **A KEY CAP IS DRAWN AS THIS COMPUTER'S KEYBOARD PRINTS IT: ON A MAC `Ctrl` IS ⌘, ⌃ ONLY WITH TAB, AND RETURN AND DELETE ARE APPLE'S OWN MARKS (`ui/widgets.rs` `printed_key`, 2026-11-13).** The strings keep naming keys as a Windows keyboard does, and one function turns them. Full account: [docs/macos.md](../../docs/macos.md)
- **ON A MAC THE LIVE PANE'S FOUR SWITCHES HAVE COMMAND KEYS BESIDE THEIR F KEYS: T NO TEXT, B BLACK, L LOGO, O LIVE, AFTER PROCLAIM'S AND FREESHOW'S MAC KEYS, BECAUSE A MAC'S TOP ROW NEEDS FN HELD (`main.rs` `mac_switch`, `ui/widgets.rs` `switch_key`, 2026-11-13, owner-directed: use what is usual on a Mac).** Full account: [docs/macos.md](../../docs/macos.md)
- **NO REAL SONG LYRICS IN THE REPOSITORY (2026-11-10).** Practice songs and test text are written for Artosia; the church's library stays in gitignored `fixtures/`. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **A REMOVAL OFFERS UNDO IN A TOAST; RED MEANS LIVE AND YELLOW MEANS HIDDEN OR WARNING (2026-11-10).** Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **A TEXT FIELD'S `on_edit` ACCESSOR NEVER CREATES STATE (`ui/editor.rs`, `edit_of`, 2026-11-10).** A field hears its own blur after its screen closed; an accessor that made a fresh editor reopened an empty "New song" over the song just saved. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **CHECK A CHANGE WITH `tools/snap.sh <folder>`, `--query "<text>"` OR A `--script` FLOW (2026-11-10).** Scenes are `SCENES` in `main.rs`; headless runs use the practice library unless `--data` names a folder. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **SEARCH RESULTS ARE ORDERED BY A CASCADE, NEVER A BLENDED SCORE (`artosia-core/src/palette.rs`, `search.rs` `Facts`, 2026-11-10, after the owner's house-kit).** A command reaches the top only when its own name is typed from its start. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **DIGITS AND SECTION LETTERS JUMP INSIDE THE LIVE SONG; ANY OTHER LETTER STARTS A SEARCH (`main.rs` `on_key`, `state.rs` `jump_to_marker`, 2026-11-11, owner-asked).** Settings switches the letters off, and every key is listed in the `?` sheet.
