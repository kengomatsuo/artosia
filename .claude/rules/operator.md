# The operator window (2026-11-10)

- **MAIN-WINDOW BUTTONS NEVER TAKE KEYBOARD FOCUS (`crates/artosia/src/ui/widgets.rs`, `Btn`, 2026-11-10).** Rune presses the focused control on Space, and Space must always mean "next slide"; dialogs and editors ask for focus with `.focusable()`. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **EVERY STRING A VOLUNTEER READS IS IN `strings.rs`, WRITTEN IN ENGLISH AND INDONESIAN; ANY OTHER LANGUAGE IS A TABLE IN `languages.rs`'S REGISTRY (2026-11-11, owner-directed).** Counted nouns go through a function; a new text changes `docs/i18n/brief.json` first and is written for each language from the brief; no language ships until a person who reads it has reviewed it. Tests fail on a dash, a middot, a known-wrong Indonesian spelling, Indonesian that differs from `docs/i18n/id.json`, or a text that differs from `crates/artosia/testdata`. Full account: [docs/i18n/README.md](../../docs/i18n/README.md)
- **NO REAL SONG LYRICS IN THE REPOSITORY (2026-11-10).** Practice songs and test text are written for Artosia; the church's library stays in gitignored `fixtures/`. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **A REMOVAL OFFERS UNDO IN A TOAST; RED MEANS LIVE AND YELLOW MEANS HIDDEN OR WARNING (2026-11-10).** Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **A TEXT FIELD'S `on_edit` ACCESSOR NEVER CREATES STATE (`ui/editor.rs`, `edit_of`, 2026-11-10).** A field hears its own blur after its screen closed; an accessor that made a fresh editor reopened an empty "New song" over the song just saved. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **CHECK A CHANGE WITH `tools/snap.sh <folder>`, `--query "<text>"` OR A `--script` FLOW (2026-11-10).** Scenes are `SCENES` in `main.rs`; headless runs use the practice library unless `--data` names a folder. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **SEARCH RESULTS ARE ORDERED BY A CASCADE, NEVER A BLENDED SCORE (`artosia-core/src/palette.rs`, `search.rs` `Facts`, 2026-11-10, after the owner's house-kit).** A command reaches the top only when its own name is typed from its start. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **DIGITS AND SECTION LETTERS JUMP INSIDE THE LIVE SONG; ANY OTHER LETTER STARTS A SEARCH (`main.rs` `on_key`, `state.rs` `jump_to_marker`, 2026-11-11, owner-asked).** Settings switches the letters off, and every key is listed in the `?` sheet. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **ONLY ONE ARTOSIA RUNS ON A LIBRARY (`main.rs` `claim_instance`, port 47824, 2026-11-10, owner-reported).** Two share one plan and one projector; the owner had two open when the Screens dialog stuck on "Starting". Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **A COLOUR ON THE WINDOW GOES THROUGH `theme` (`ink_of`, `tint_of`, `warn_ink`, `accent`), BECAUSE THE WINDOW IS LIGHT OR DARK (2026-11-10, owner-reported).** Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **A CHURCH'S OWN FILES (BULLETIN, BIRTHDAY LIST) NEVER ENTER THE REPOSITORY (2026-11-10).** They hold names, birthdays and gifts; tests build their own files. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **A NEW SCREEN IS RENDERED IN INDONESIAN TOO (`--indonesian`), BECAUSE ITS WORDS ARE LONGER (2026-11-10).** Three layouts clipped the first time. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **THE WINDOW IS THE PLAN, THEN PREVIEW AND LIVE SIDE BY SIDE (`ui/shell.rs`, 2026-11-10, the owner's pick from four trials).** Live stays in view beside an editor, and a live slide kept off the projector is yellow, never red. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **PLAIN ARROWS MOVE THE PROJECTOR, AS IN EASISLIDES; SHIFT PICKS IN THE LIVE SONG AND SHOWS ON RELEASE; CTRL MOVES THE RING IN PREVIEW (`main.rs` `on_key`, 2026-11-11, owner-directed).** Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **PREVIEW IS WHAT COMES NEXT (`state.rs` `queue_next`, `preview_waits`, 2026-11-10, owner-directed).** Artosia queues the next plan item there and never replaces what a person opened; Next at the end of the live item shows it at its ring. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **ONLY SHOW, ENTER, NEXT AND THE LIVE LIST SEND TO THE PROJECTOR; A CLICK IN PREVIEW, ON A PLAN ROW OR ON A SEARCH RESULT NEVER DOES (`state.rs` `select_at`, 2026-11-11, owner-directed).** Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **SHOW FILLS THE LIVE LIST AND THE LIVE SWITCH (F6) DECIDES WHETHER THE ROOM SEES IT; THE SWITCH IS ON AT EVERY START AND NEVER SAVED (`state.rs` `toggle_live`, `off_air`, 2026-11-11, owner-directed).** Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **TILES OF ONE ROW ARE ONE SIZE TAKEN FROM THE STRIP, NEVER FROM A WORD; A STATE IS A LIT TILE NAMED BY WHAT IS TRUE; EVERY BUTTON HAS AN ICON AND PRINTS ITS KEY AS THE KEYBOARD DOES (`ui/widgets.rs` `Tile`, `keycaps`, 2026-11-11, owner-directed).** Full account: [docs/ux-principles.md](../../docs/ux-principles.md)
- **DESIGN HOLDS WHAT THE PROJECTOR DRAWS AND SETTINGS HOLDS THE PROGRAM'S OWN; NOTHING THAT IS PART OF THE WORK GOES IN SETTINGS (`ui/sheets.rs` `looks`, `settings`, 2026-11-11, owner-reported).** Full account: [docs/ux-principles.md](../../docs/ux-principles.md)
- **A KEY OR POINTER CHANGE IS ALSO PRESSED IN A REAL WINDOW WITH `tools/drive-window.ps1` (2026-11-11).** Shift let go did nothing in the real window while every headless script passed. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **A CHURCH HAS ONE PLAN PER SERVICE, AND SWITCHING NEVER TOUCHES THE PROJECTOR (`plans.rs` `enter_plan`, `ui/plans.rs`, 2026-11-11, owner-asked).** Item ids belong to one plan, so a switch re-finds the live item by its song in the new plan and Next starts the new plan from its top otherwise. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **A PLAN MAY CARRY ITS OWN LOOK, AND EVERY SCREEN READS `App::look()`, NEVER `settings.look` (`plans.rs`, `Plan.look`, `ui/look.rs` `chooser`, 2026-11-11, owner's example: camera in the morning, none in Sunday school).** A switch of plan keeps the live words and changes the whole look under them. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **THE FIRST START RUNS A FIVE-STOP TOUR THAT OWNS THE KEYBOARD AND CAN BE SKIPPED AND STARTED AGAIN (`ui/tour.rs`, `main.rs` `on_key`, 2026-11-11, owner-asked).** It teaches the plan, Preview, Show, Live and search, never keys; its ring goes round a keyed container, never one button. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
- **THE SEARCH LEARNS FROM USE, AND A HABIT ONLY ORDERS SONGS THAT TIED ON EVERYTHING ELSE (`artosia-core/src/learn.rs`, `search.rs` `Facts.habit`, `learning.rs`, 2026-11-11, owner-approved).** Nothing is recorded in practice, headless runs or with the switch off, and never what is typed into a message, countdown or stream key. Full account: [docs/palette-suggestions.md](../../docs/palette-suggestions.md)
- **ARTOSIA OFFERS WHAT TO SHOW NEXT IN ONE STRIP, AND AN OFFER ONLY EVER FILLS PREVIEW (`suggest.rs`, `ui/suggest.rs`, F4 takes the first, 2026-11-11, owner-asked).** A misheard word must not reach the room; the "not on the screen" line needs proof the listener reads the room and two misses in a row. Full account: [docs/palette-suggestions.md](../../docs/palette-suggestions.md)
- **A SONG'S SECTIONS ARE PROPOSED, NEVER WRITTEN, UNTIL A PRESS (`artosia-core/src/marks.rs`, `ui/editor.rs` `marks_strip`, `App::apply_marks`, 2026-11-11, owner-asked).** Paste, the web and Tidy leave words with no heading unmarked so the strip can offer chorus, bridge and numbered verses; applying is one undoable edit. Full account: [docs/operator.md](../../docs/operator.md)
