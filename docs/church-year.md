# The church year in Artosia (SEASONS1, 2026-11-15)

The owner asked for themed templates for Christmas, Easter ("the Rise of Jesus") and the other Christian events, and whether they should switch by themselves. Answer built: both. Each template family carries colourways tagged with a season, a person can pick one by hand, and an opt-in setting, Follow the church year, swaps every card to its family's colourway for today's season. It is off by default because a church may keep one look all year.
